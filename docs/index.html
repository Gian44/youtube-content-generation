SLEEP ON FACTS

Calm facts to fall asleep to

Sleep On Facts is a YouTube channel of long, slow, softly narrated videos about one subject at a time — whales, deserts, the night sky — made to be played while you drift off.

About the app

"sleeponfacts" is the small automation that produces and uploads those videos. It runs on a schedule, writes a narration from public sources, reads it with a text-to-speech voice, lays it over licence-free stock photographs and a quiet synthesised soundtrack, and uploads the result to the channel. The narration is AI-generated; every video says so in its description.

The code is open: github.com/Gian44/youtube-content-generation.
