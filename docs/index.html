SLEEP ON FACTS

Calm facts to fall asleep to

Sleep On Facts is a YouTube channel of long, slow, softly narrated videos about one subject at a time — Saturn, the Indus Valley, the Great Barrier Reef — made to be played while you drift off.

About the app

"sleeponfacts" is the small automation that produces and uploads those videos. It runs on a schedule, researches each subject on Wikipedia, writes a narration from that research, reads it with a text-to-speech voice, and lays it over photographs of the subject from Wikimedia Commons, NASA and licence-free stock libraries, with a quiet synthesised soundtrack. The narration is AI-generated; every video says so in its description.

Photo credits

Every video's description links to its own credits page listing each photograph shown, its author, source and licence. The pages live under /credits/ on this site.

The code is open: github.com/Gian44/youtube-content-generation.
